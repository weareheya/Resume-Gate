# Profile

The facts a resume states outside Claim Cards. Fill it in during Step 0 from your current resume, and confirm every row yourself. `scripts/check_claims.py` checks each role heading in Experience (`### Title · Company · Years`) and each Education line against these tables: the title and company (or degree and school) must appear in the line, and every year in the line must be in that row. Punctuation and case don't matter ("B.A." matches "BA"), and a cell can list accepted variants separated by `;`, e.g. `Partnerships Manager; 合作伙伴经理`. Until you fill it in, these lines simply aren't checked.
