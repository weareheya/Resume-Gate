# C-001 · Grew channel partners from 12 to 40 and rebuilt the tiering

## What happened (raw, unpolished)
Northwind's channel partners came mostly from the founder's personal contacts, with no standards. I set up a three-tier model (strategic / growth / long tail), wrote entry and renewal criteria, and reached out to 60+ candidates, of whom 28 signed. After tiering, strategic partners got a dedicated contact, and the long tail moved to self-serve onboarding.

## Numbers and what they mean
| Number | Unit / currency | Time window | Basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| 12 → 40 active partners | partners | 2022.03–2023.09 | "Active" = at least one deal-related lead in the past 90 days |
| Partner-sourced share of new leads, 18% → 34% | % | same | Share of all new leads, not share of closed revenue |

## Attribution
- **What I did**: the tiering design, the entry criteria, the candidate list, and negotiating the first 20 signings.
- **What the team or others did**: a new colleague took over the last 8 signings; engineering built the partner portal.
- **Causal claims I can't make**: brand campaigns ran at the same time, so the rise in lead share isn't entirely down to the partner program.

## Approved wording
> Rebuilt the partner program into a three-tier model and grew active partners from 12 to 40 in 18 months; partner-sourced leads rose from 18% to 34% of new pipeline.

## Don't rephrase as
- Don't write "drove $X in revenue." There's no closed-revenue evidence.
- "40" is active partners, not signed partners. (The 28 signed are the ones I handled personally.)

CRM export (2023.09), the quarterly partner review deck, and a former manager who can confirm it.
