# C-003 · Partner-sourced pipeline reached US$2.1M

## What happened (raw, unpolished)
After the tiering (C-001), I set up deal registration so partners logged the opportunities they brought. By year end, open and closed opportunities registered by partners added up to US$2.1M. Most were still open when I left; I don't know how many closed.

## Numbers and what they mean
| Number | Unit / currency | Time window | Basis | Verified |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| US$2.1M partner-sourced pipeline | USD | FY2023 | Total value of partner-registered opportunities, open and closed. **Pipeline, not revenue** | ✓ |

## Attribution
- **What I did**: designed deal registration and chased partners to log their deals.
- **What the team or others did**: the sales team worked the deals.
- **Causal claims I can't make**: no closed-revenue number, so nothing about revenue or bookings.

## Approved wording
> Set up partner deal registration; partner-sourced pipeline reached US$2.1M in FY2023.

## Don't rephrase as
- Banned words: revenue, bookings, sales, closed
- Don't write "generated US$2.1M in revenue" or "closed US$2.1M". It's pipeline.
- Don't round up to "US$2M+ in sales".

## Source
CRM pipeline report, filtered to partner-registered opportunities (exported 2023.12).

## Notes
If asked about close rate, say you left before most deals closed.
