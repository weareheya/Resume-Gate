# C-004 · Ran four joint webinars with three strategic partners

## What happened (raw, unpolished)
I planned and ran four co-branded webinars with three strategic-tier partners: picked the topics, split the promotion, and handled the follow-up lists. Together they drew 380 registrants. These were marketing events; nobody sold jointly on a deal.

| 4 webinars | events | 2023.02–2023.08 | Co-branded, co-promoted | ✓ |
| 380 registrants | people | same | Total registrations across all four, not attendees | ✓ |

## Attribution
- **What I did**: topics, partner coordination, promotion plan, follow-up.
- **What the team or others did**: the marketing team made the landing pages and ran the email sends.
- **Causal claims I can't make**: no tracking from registrant to deal.
